QUICKSTART

One workflow. Continuous confidence.

Drop this into your repository and let every pull request prove its accessibility.

.github/workflows/accesslint.yml
name: AccessLint
on: [pull_request]

jobs:
  accessibility:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - uses: Shashank163007/Team-ignite-accesslint@main
        with:
          github-token: ${{ secrets.GITHUB_TOKEN }}
